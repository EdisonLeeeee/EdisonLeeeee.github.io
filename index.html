About me

I will join the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Xiamen University as an Assistant Professor in November 2025. I received my M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from Sun Yat-sen University in 2021 and 2025, respectively, where I was advised by Prof. Liang Chen and Prof. Zibin Zheng.

My research interests include:

  • Trustworthy Graph Learning: reliability, fairness, etc.

  • Graph Neural Networks

  • Generative Recommendation

  • Large Language Models

PyTorch Geometric

 

I am a core maintainer of PyTorch Geometric (PyG), Feel free to reach out to me for support or guidance in working with PyG.
